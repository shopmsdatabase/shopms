01At the counter

Bill a customer in seconds

Tap the product or scan its barcode. Take cash, card or digital payment, split it, or put the rest on account. Hold a bill when someone steps away and pick it up later.

  • Barcode, batch and serial search
  • Hold and resume bills, multiple tills
  • Discounts, VAT and change handled
02Behind the shelf

Stock counts itself, down to colour and size

Most systems tell you "38 shirts". That is useless when a customer asks for red in large. ShopMS counts every variant separately — through purchases, sales, returns and transfers.

  • Colour, size and custom variants
  • Batch and serial tracking
  • Low stock flagged before it runs out
03Money owed

Every due against a name and an invoice

Part payment today, the rest next week — it lands in that customer's ledger with a running balance. Same for what you owe suppliers. Nobody has to remember anything.

  • Debit and credit per invoice
  • Customer and supplier ledgers
  • Export any ledger to PDF or CSV
04No internet, no problem

Fully offline, and your devices still talk to each other

This is the part most shop software gets wrong. ShopMS does not just survive offline — your counter machine, your phone and a second till keep each other updated with no internet at all. When the connection returns, everything syncs to the cloud.

  • Sell, receive stock and print with no connection
  • Devices sync directly to each other
  • Cloud backup queues until you are online
05Closing time

The day adds itself up

Sales, purchases, expenses, staff payroll, what is in the bank and what is in the drawer — totalled before you lock the shutter. And the month's best sellers, so you know what to reorder.

  • Daily and monthly reports
  • Payroll, expenses and profit
  • Best sellers and slow movers
